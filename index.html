Adventurous Beginnings

Gray launched her firm in 2004. She designs from her studio in Santa Fe while often visiting her weaving partners in Nepal and India. The end result of this worldwide enterprise is a collection of vibrant and original handmade contemporary rugs made with only the highest quality materials, and the utmost ethical standards.
